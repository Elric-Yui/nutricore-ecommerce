# Design System: BioLabs Noir (The Synthetic Alchemist) This document serves as the technical specification for the "BioLabs Noir" design system, optimized for high-performance supplement e-commerce. ## 1. Creative North Star: "The Synthetic Alchemist" The aesthetic combines clinical precision with high-fashion editorial. It's dark, authoritative, and elite. ## 2. Visual Foundation ### Color Palette - **Primary:** `#A3E635` (Neon Green/Lime) - Used for primary CTAs, active states, and high-impact accents. - **Surface (Deep):** `#131313` - Main background for pages and sections. - **Surface (Container):** `#1c1b1b` - Used for cards, sections, and structural separation. - **Text (High Emphasis):** `#FFFFFF` - Headings and primary body text. - **Text (Medium Emphasis):** `#c2cab0` - Secondary information, labels, and inactive nav. - **Borders/Lines:** `#353535` - Low-opacity strokes for subtle structure. ### Typography - **Headlines:** `Noto Serif` - Used for high-impact, editorial-style titles. High contrast, tight letter-spacing. - **Body & Labels:** `Space Grotesk` - Technical, monospaced-adjacent feel for functional UI, data points, and metadata. - **Scale:** - Display: 64px+ - Heading 1: 48px - Heading 2: 32px - Body: 16px - Label: 12px (Uppercase, tracked out) ### Shape & Structure - **Roundness:** `4px` (Small radius) - Maintains a sharp, surgical feel. - **Spacing:** Generous vertical whitespace. Sections are separated by large gutters to create an "archive" or "lab report" feel. - **Borders:** 1px solid borders are preferred over shadows for depth. ## 3. Shared Components ### Top Navigation Bar - **Logo:** `BIOLABS NOIR` (Noto Serif, Bold, Uppercase, Tracking-tighter) - **Links:** Shop, Science, About, Support (Space Grotesk, Uppercase) - **Actions:** Cart icon, Account icon. - **Style:** Fixed, blur background (`backdrop-blur-xl`), subtle bottom border. ### Footer - **Layout:** 4-column grid (Brand info, Navigation, Support, Newsletter). - **Style:** Dark surface, small uppercase labels, copyright line at the bottom. ### Buttons - **Primary:** Solid `#A3E635` background, black text, bold, uppercase. - **Secondary:** Transparent with `#A3E635` border or white text. ## 4. Design Patterns - **Product Cards:** Dark background, high-quality 3D renders, neon green labels for status (e.g., "NEW RELEASE", "IN STOCK"). - **Legal/Technical Content:** Numbered sections (01, 02, etc.) in Space Grotesk to emphasize clinical rigor. - **Data Visualization:** Use bar charts or percentage indicators with neon green fills to show "Bioavailability" or "Clinical Results". ## 5.
